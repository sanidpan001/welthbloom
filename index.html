What is Step-up SIP (Top-up SIP)?

Step-up SIP is also called Top-up SIP or Increasing SIP. In this, you increase your SIP amount every year by fixed percentage like 10%. If you start with ₹10,000 per month in 2026, in 2027 you invest ₹11,000, in 2028 ₹12,100, and so on. Mutual fund houses like SBI MF, HDFC MF, ICICI Prudential allow auto step-up. Our WealthBloom calculator calculates exact maturity with compounding of increasing SIP. Formula is complex: Future Value = Sum of [SIP * (1+step%)^year * FV factor]. But we do it in 0.001 sec.
